A letter from my heart

Dear Angeline,

There is something I've wanted to tell you for quite some time. I kept wondering whether I should say it, but I decided that sometimes the heart deserves to be honest.

I've been wanting to say this for a while, but I never really knew how to start.

There's something about you that caught my attention. Maybe it's the way you talk, the way you smile, or simply the way you carry yourself. I can't really point to one exact moment when it happened, but somewhere along the way, I started becoming interested in you.

I like you.

And honestly, I don't want to pretend that I already know everything about you. In fact, that's one of the things I like most about this feeling. I want to know you.

I want to know the little things about you. What makes you happy, what makes you laugh, the things you're passionate about, the things you dream about, and even the random little things that most people might not notice.

I want to hear your stories. I want to know what kind of music you like, what your favorite things are, what you do when you're having a bad day, and what makes an ordinary day a good one for you.

I'm not writing this because I expect anything from you.

I just wanted to be honest about how I feel.

Maybe this could become something more, or maybe it won't. I don't know. And that's okay. I just know that I'd really like the chance to get to know you better not just as someone I like, but as the person you really are.

So, if you're comfortable with it...

I'd really like to get to know you.

With a little courage and a lot of sincerity...
